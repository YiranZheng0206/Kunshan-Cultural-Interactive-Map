What this prototype can—and cannot—show

01

Dataset coverage

Counts describe 82 records assembled for this course project. They do not represent every cultural resource in Kunshan.

02

Documented relationships

A network line means one represented relationship with a listed source. Its length does not measure strength, distance, or causality.

03

Route heuristics

Route options compare simple rules and straight-line distance. They are not live travel, traffic, opening-hour, or accessibility guidance.
